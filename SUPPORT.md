# Getting help

* **Setup, configuration, deployment:** read the [README](README.md) first (Quick start, "Upload storage: local or PostFile",
  "Deploying to Vercel"). Most questions are answered there.
* **Something is broken:** search the existing issues, then open a *Bug report* with steps to reproduce.
* **Idea or missing feature:** open a *Feature request*.
* **Security problem:** do not open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
* **Commercial use or licensing:** email the project owner at the address on his GitHub profile ([RealUnfazed](https://github.com/RealUnfazed); see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

Aurelune is a volunteer, non-commercial project, so replies can take a few days. Please be patient and kind
([Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).
